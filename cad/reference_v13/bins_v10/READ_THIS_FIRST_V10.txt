WHAT THIS IS
Two tiers of 8 wedge bins around the scoop yaw axis, one 22.5 deg slot each,
so the scoop turns the same step for every bin. BJ 2026-11-03 12:34 PT: each
floor standardizes to its biggest size; the only difference is floor 1 vs 2.
So there are just two bin types, and every mouth is the same width:
  Floor 1 (lower, bricks), 8 x part 40: walls 140, inner wall (sill) 70. The
    opening above the sill is the receiving window; the upper carrier sits 10
    above the rim.
  Floor 2 (upper, other parts), 8 x part 41: walls 140, open top (the old L).
The allocation's S/M/L classes no longer change any geometry.
Geometric volume: floor 1 0.68 L each to the sill, floor 2 1.29 L each to
10 below the rim. Floor 2 share 65% (allocation target 60%). These are
geometric numbers before packing voids, not measured LEGO loose-fill. Do the
loose-fill measurement from ALLOCATION_UPDATE before trusting any capacity.

FOOTPRINT
Both tiers 596 wide x 298 deep from the yaw axis (lip edge), so they fit the
600 x 350 target with 52 mm for the carrier front and runners. Stack 320 tall
from the lower carrier top. Bins 6 mm apart. Every bin and locator was
intersected with every other one: no clashes.

PARTS (all fit a 248 x 248 bed; single solid, watertight, checked)
40 floor 1 bin x8      106 x 148 x 140   print upright, open top
41 floor 2 bin x8      106 x 148 x 140   print upright, open top
42 bin locator x16     114 x 147 x 16.5  print flat, bottom on bed
43 compact scoop x1    print flat; 252 long, so rotate it about 45 deg on
                       the bed (fits at 236 x 236)
Bin walls and floor 2.4 (same as test bins V1/V2). No supports: the
deflector and grip lip undersides are 45 deg.

BIN FEATURES (selections N and P1)
- Integral receiving mouth (P1): the inner wall is the sill, with a 45 deg
  deflector inside it so a part that clips the edge slides into the bin.
- Easy grip for kids (N): an 8 mm grip lip around the outer wall top. Grab the
  lip from outside with fingers over the rim. No latch, no tool, no bar.
- Shallow locating pocket (N): each bin drops into its own locator, a 3 mm
  pocket with 0.6 mm clearance per side. Loose on purpose so kids can lift
  bins out. Runbook fits (0.07 sliding, -0.02 press) are for joints, not this.
- Presence switch: a pocket in each locator holds a KW11-3Z roller-lever
  switch upright, lever up through a 4.6 x 24 window. The empty bin's weight
  presses the roller. Wires run in a 6 x 4 groove to the inner edge.
  The switch body size is NOT measured: SW_L 20, SW_W 6.4, SW_H 10.3 are
  typical values. Measure one of your 40 switches with calipers and edit SW_*
  before printing 16 locators. The carrier underneath traps the switch.
- Three M3 countersunk holes per locator screw it down to the carrier
  (carrier holes and inserts are not designed yet).

COMPACT SCOOP (part 43, replaces V3 part 05, same hub 06 and 4 M3 screws)
110 wide catch area under the scanner tapering to a 40 wide outlet, 40 deg
floor, outlet floor at r 135. Everything is trimmed inside r 137 so the scoop
can turn and move up/down at any height without touching a bin or locator
(locators start at r 149, bins at r 152). Checked at all 16 delivery poses:
no clashes. Outlet 40 vs bin mouth 49.5: 4.2 mm margin after 1 deg yaw error
each side. Parts wider than 40 across do not fit the outlet: the 6x8 plate
(48 wide) needs an exclusion or a wider outlet.

PART FLIGHT (estimate, not tested)
The part leaves the outlet and has to cross 17.5 mm to the bin wall while
dropping 39 mm. Estimated exit speed 0.8 to 1.2 m/s (friction 0.6 to 0.3),
which crosses 31 to 36 mm. Below about 0.45 m/s a part could drop into the
centre column under the scoop. Test with real parts before printing 16 bins.

MUST CHANGE ELSEWHERE
1 Lift travel: delivery heights need 236 mm of lift travel (lowest scoop point
  from 118.5 at the lower bins to 355 at the floor 2 bins, both measured from
  the lower carrier top). A 30 mm tall part on the outlet clears the upper
  carrier by 10.8 mm at the lower tier. The V3 lift CAD
  has 105 (H 95 to 200). The 350 mm rods and 300 mm T8 screw in the BOM may
  allow it; the tower, brace and switch mounts need a taller version. Or make
  the floor 2 bins shorter (each 10 mm off saves 10 mm of travel).
2 Carriers: U-shaped trays with the r 150 cutout from V5, flat under the
  locators, with M3 holes for the locators. Not designed here.
3 Filament: CAD solid volume is about 2.8 L for the 16 bins (walls are nearly
  solid at 4 loops) and 2.7 L for the 16 locators (mostly infill). Expect
  roughly 3 to 4 kg of PLA; slice to confirm. This is far above the BOM line.

PRINT ORDER (runbook rule: test small first)
1 Measure a KW11-3Z switch; edit SW_*; print ONE locator.
2 Print ONE floor 1 bin and ONE floor 2 bin. Check the drop-in fit, the lip grip
  and that an empty bin presses the switch.
3 Print the compact scoop and slide 20 real parts down it into a bin by hand.
4 Only then batch the rest.
