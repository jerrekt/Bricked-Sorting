BRICKED SORTING - V13: STATIC DRUM OUTLET PLATE + HELICAL FLIGHT
2026-11-05 PT. Units mm. Made by Claude in CadQuery on top of V12 (read
READ_THIS_FIRST_V12.txt first; everything there still holds, same body
1040 x 520 x 1248 plus the hopper). PROPOSED, NOT A PRINT RELEASE.
Scripts: build_drum_v13.py (the two new drum parts, in the V7 barrel frame),
build_machine_v13.py (V12 plus the drum swap) and render_machine_v13.py.
Checks: machine_checks.json and isolate_v13/drum_v13_checks.json.

WHAT CHANGED (BJ 2026-11-04: "the circle at the end of the tumbler should be
static. the inside should be a spiral to help push it forward")
47b TUMBLER BARREL WITH HELICAL FLIGHT replaces 47 (three straight ribs).
    Same 160 OD x 152 ID x 180 tube, same three end bosses and M3 inserts, so
    the drive core 54, tire 55, rollers 50 and cradles 52 are unchanged.
    Inside: one triangular flight, 12 tall x 12 wide at the wall, pitch 90,
    1.75 turns from z 8 to z 165. Underside is 45 deg, so it prints with the
    axis vertical and no supports. Every turn of the drum walks a brick 90
    forward; at 4 rpm that is 6 mm/s, about 30 s end to end, while the
    tumbling still separates the pieces. 410 cm3 of PLA.
49b STATIC OUTLET PLATE replaces the rotating eccentric cap 49 AND the
    thrust holder 57. One printed part, 172 dia x 26, face down on the bed:
    - face plate 4 thick at the barrel's front end, 1 mm off the barrel,
    - inner spill lip 8 wide reaching 6 mm into the bore (r 58..67) so
      pieces cannot fall out between barrel and plate, cut away over the
      outlet,
    - thrust ring r 68..80 that the barrel's front bosses run against (the
      plate carries the end load; the 9th 625 bearing on 57 is gone),
    - outlet window 65 wide, r 28..68, at the bottom (the barrel's +X, down
      side). The window's inner edge sits 8 mm in from the wall, so a brick
      has to ride up the flight and tip over the lip: nothing falls out on
      its own, the spiral has to deliver it.
    - two ears, one per cradle: 8 x 40 tab on the top of the plate and a
      4 mm strip on the cradle's inner face (y 83.5..87.5 from the drum
      axis), two M3 each, through the cradle plate. Print the ears at 100%
      walls; the whole drum's thrust goes through them.
DROPPED: 49 eccentric cap, 57 thrust holder, one 625 bearing (9 -> 8).
NO OTHER PART MOVED. Cradles, rollers, motor bridge, chute and hopper are the
V12 files; the chute still aims into the barrel's rear end.

PRINT ORDER (runbook: test small first)
1 49b plate alone: check the ears on the cradles and the window edge.
2 47b barrel (axis vertical, outlet end down, about 7 h): spin by hand on the
  rollers against the plate, then drop ten mixed bricks in and time them.
3 If pieces take too long, cut TURNS to 1.25; if they shoot through, 2.25.
